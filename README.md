This project applies RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) Analysis to a real-world retail dataset to identify customer segments and provide actionable business recommendations. The goal is to help marketing and business teams understand customer behavior, improve retention strategies, and maximize revenue through targeted campaigns.

Using transaction data from an online retail business, customers were segmented into groups such as Champions, Loyal Customers, Potential Loyalists, At Risk, and Lost Customers based on their purchasing patterns.

Business Problem

Retail businesses often struggle to answer critical questions such as:

Who are our most valuable customers?
Which customers are likely to stop purchasing?
Where should marketing efforts be focused?
How can customer retention be improved?

This project addresses these challenges by leveraging RFM analysis to create meaningful customer segments and generate data-driven recommendations.

Data Cleaning

The following preprocessing steps were performed:

Removed records with missing Customer IDs
Identified and removed cancelled transactions
Removed negative quantity transactions (returns/refunds)
Created Revenue feature:
Revenue = Quantity * UnitPrice
Validated data quality before analysis
RFM Analysis

Three customer metrics were calculated:

Recency (R)

Number of days since the customer's most recent purchase.

Frequency (F)

Number of unique purchases made by the customer.

Monetary (M)

Total revenue generated by the customer.

Each metric was scored on a scale of 1–5 using quintile-based segmentation.

Customer Segments

Customers were classified into the following groups:

Segment	Description
Champions	Most valuable and highly engaged customers
Loyal Customers	Frequent purchasers with strong engagement
Potential Loyalists	Customers showing signs of becoming loyal
At Risk	Previously active customers with declining engagement
Lost Customers	Customers with low engagement and long inactivity
Other	Customers not fitting the main categories

Key Insights
Champions Drive Revenue

Although Champions represent approximately 22% of customers, they generate the majority of total revenue.

Customer Retention Opportunity

The At Risk segment contributes over £819K in revenue, highlighting an opportunity for targeted retention campaigns.

Growth Potential

Potential Loyalists represent customers who could be converted into higher-value segments through personalized marketing strategies.
